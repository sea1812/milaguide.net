Cloud costs can quickly spiral out of control. Here are proven strategies to optimize your spending.

## 1. Right-Size Your Instances

Many companies overprovision their cloud resources. Start by analyzing actual usage patterns.

## 2. Use Reserved Instances

For steady-state workloads, reserved instances can save up to 60% compared to on-demand pricing.

## 3. Implement Auto-Scaling

Scale your infrastructure based on actual demand, not peak capacity.

## 4. Clean Up Orphaned Resources

Regularly audit and remove unattached volumes, unused IP addresses, and stale snapshots.

## 5. Leverage Spot Instances

For batch processing and fault-tolerant workloads, spot instances can offer 90% discounts.

## 6. Use Lifecycle Policies

Automate the transition of data to cheaper storage tiers.

## 7. Choose the Right Region

Consider latency and pricing when selecting regions for your workloads.

## 8. Monitor and Alert

Set up budget alerts before costs become a surprise.

## 9. Use Managed Services Wisely

Sometimes managed services are more expensive than self-hosting for stable workloads.

## 10. Review Bills Regularly

Make cloud cost review a regular part of your operations.
